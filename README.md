# Auto Mutant Turtle

Defeat enemies and puzzle their DNA into your turtle to grow stronger so you can take down The Fleshmancer.

## About
**Auto Mutant Turtle** was created for the [**Godot Wild Jam #94**](https://itch.io/jam/godot-wild-jam-94) in 9 days by a team of 5.

You can play the game here: [Auto Mutant Turtle](https://arko-poet.itch.io/auto-mutant-turtle)
